The Solution: Zimmer Biomet GCC Workspace Platform

A lightning-fast, progressive web application accessible from any phone, laptop, or tablet.

🗺️ Interactive CAD Map

Real architectural vector floor plan. See 184 desks with live availability tooltips without guessing. Reserve in 1 click.

🍽️ Cafeteria & Lifestyle

Daily meal cards with diet filters (Veg, Non-veg, Egg), chef specials, and star ratings. Pinned announcements & 1-tap event RSVPs.

🛠️ Instant Facilities Portal

Scan desk/room QR stickers to report problems with photos. Dedicated "Staff Home" portal with big buttons for on-ground workers.

📊 Leadership Intelligence

Real-time analytics on peak occupancy, most utilized zones, and room hours. Data-driven real estate planning.

🛡️ Enterprise Governance

Custom roles builder (Facility Manager, Staff, HR, Employee) with fine-grained permissions and full audit logging.

⚡ Serverless Economics

Deployed on Cloudflare Workers edge network with Neon PostgreSQL. Sub-50ms latency, zero server patching.
